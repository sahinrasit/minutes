Your meetings never leave your phone.

Record, transcribe and summarize meetings entirely on your device. No cloud, no bot, no minute quota, and a one-time price.

  • Live transcript while you type your own notes, side by side or top and bottom.

Help & FAQ

Does my audio go to a server?

No. Recording, transcription and summaries all run on your iPhone or iPad using Apple's on-device speech and language models. Minutes has no server.

Why don't I get a summary?

Summaries use Apple Intelligence. Your device must support it and it must be turned on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri. Without it, Minutes still saves the full transcript and your notes.

Which languages are transcribed?

English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean and Chinese use Apple's long-form speech model. Other languages, such as Turkish and Dutch, use on-device system dictation where available. Choose the language in Settings → Transcription. The first time, the speech model for your language is downloaded from Apple.

Can I record a Zoom or Teams call?

Minutes listens through the microphone in the room. It does not join calls as a bot. Put the call on speaker if you want both sides in the transcript.

Do I need permission to record?

Recording laws differ by country and state, and some require the consent of every participant. Always tell everyone that you are recording.

What does Minutes Pro include?

Unlimited meetings with summaries, action items and export. Choose a one-time Lifetime purchase or a monthly subscription. The free version includes three meetings.

I reinstalled the app. How do I get Pro back?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel the monthly plan?

Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone and choose Minutes.
