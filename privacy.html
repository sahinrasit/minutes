Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Minutes records, transcribes and summarizes meetings. It is designed so that your recordings, transcripts and notes never leave your device.

Recordings, transcripts and notes

When you record, Minutes uses the microphone and stores the audio file in the app's private storage on your device. Speech is transcribed on your device with Apple's on-device speech recognition, and summaries, decisions and action items are generated on your device with Apple's on-device language model. Your notes, transcripts and summaries are stored on your device as well. We do not upload, read, analyse or keep copies of any of this, and Minutes has no account system and no server.

If you use iCloud Backup, your device backup can include Minutes data. That backup is stored in your own iCloud account under Apple's terms. If you delete a meeting, its recording, transcript and notes are deleted from the app. If you delete the app, all of its data is deleted with it, except for files you exported yourself.

Speech model downloads

The first time you transcribe in a language, your device may download Apple's speech model for that language from Apple. This download does not include your audio.

Reminders

If you choose Add to Reminders, Minutes asks for access to Reminders and creates reminders for your action items in your default list. Minutes does not read your other reminders.

Purchases

Minutes Pro is sold as an in-app purchase processed by Apple. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. To confirm that a purchase is valid and to restore it on your other devices, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives a random identifier generated by the app, your purchase receipt from Apple, the app version, device model and country or region. This information is used only to provide and restore your purchase and is not linked to your identity. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we do not collect

  • No audio, transcripts, summaries or notes
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No contacts, location or photos
  • No sale or sharing of personal data

Recording other people

You are responsible for recording lawfully. Recording laws differ by country and state, and some require the consent of every participant. Minutes reminds you to tell participants before each recording.

Children

Minutes does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
